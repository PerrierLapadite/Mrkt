Questions

How do I find an item in a long list?
Use Filter my items (from 7 items). The ↑ ↓ buttons, shown while you scroll, jump to the top or the bottom.
How do I give every item the same price?
Tap Same price for all… under the list title, type the price (for example 5x), then Apply.
Selling: how do I add a discount?
At the bottom of the Items tab: Discount & total. Type the % and a name. There you can also choose the total in coins, rings or blankets, and hide the ≈ rings / ≈ blankets.
Buying: what is the bulk bonus?
You pay more when someone sells you a big part of an item’s quantity in one go. At the bottom of the Items tab: Bulk bonus & total → ＋ Add a tier. Set how much is sold in one go, then how much more you pay (in %). Tap the unit to count in % of item (share of that item’s quantity) or items (number of items sold at once, all together). Add up to 4 tiers; the best one reached wins. In the Order tab the bonus is added to the price automatically, with the subtotal, the bonus and how many more items unlock the next tier. The picture shows each tier with the total for the whole list, and the Order tab adds the bonus to the lines that qualify.
What does “!” mean in the Order tab?
That item isn’t in your post, so it isn’t counted. Tap its name to pick the right item, or ignore the line.
