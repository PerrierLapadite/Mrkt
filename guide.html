03Adding items

The library holds the Hay Day products, from crops to expansion materials. You search, you tap, it is in your list.

  1. Type a few letters in the search bar: lob → Lobster Tail, rasp → Raspberry, bem → BEM Set. French names work too (blé → Wheat).
  2. Tap a product to add it. The results stay open, so you can add several in a row.
  3. Tap Done (or anywhere outside) to close the results.
  • With an empty search, browse by category: Popular, Expansion sets, Materials & tools, Crops, Animal goods, Bakery… Show more loads more results.
  • Enter on the keyboard adds the first result.
  • Already in your list? The site jumps to it instead of adding it twice.
  • Not in the library? ＋ Custom item adds an item with the name you type.
PicturesGame pictures load by themselves from the Hay Day wiki. If one can’t load, an emoji is shown instead.

Change a picture

  • Game image: search and pick another product picture. “Rename the item too” also changes its name.
  • Upload your own: a photo or a screenshot. “Use for every …” applies it to that product in all your posts.
  • Emoji: type one, then Save — or Emoji only to show just the emoji.
  • ↺ Back to the game image undoes your change.

04Trade posts

A trade post has no prices. It says what you offer, what you want, and how many you give for how many you get.

50:89
I give 50 of my item for 89 of yours.
With BEM Set under “I have” and Lemon Curd in the 50 : 89 group, the post means: 50 BEM Sets for 89 Lemon Curd. The first number is always yours, the second is theirs.

I have

  • Tap I have in “Add to”, then search your item.
  • Set your stock with − / + or type it.
  • You can offer several items. Each ratio group then shows which one you give.

Add to

Before tapping products, choose where they go:

I have 50 : 89 45 : 89 60 : 89 + Ratio

The selected group gets a red frame in your list, so you always know where new items land.

A new ratio

  • Tap + Ratio, type I give : I get, then Use this ratio. The next items you add go into this new group.
  • If you offer several items, the panel also asks which one you give.
TipAdd all the items of one ratio, then switch to the next ratio. It is faster than fixing ratios one by one.

Ratio groups

Items are grouped by ratio: one box for 50 : 89, one for 60 : 89… The picture shows the same boxes.

  1. The big numbers are the ratio. Change them and the whole group follows.
  2. The small picture on the left is the item you give. If you offer several items, tap it to switch (⇄).
  3. ⋯ of the group: add items to this group, or remove the group and its items.
  4. ★ highlights an item — it is marked in red on the picture.
  5. ⋯ of an item: change picture or ratio, add a note, move, duplicate, move to “I have”, remove.
  6. + Add items here selects this group and takes you to the search bar.
  • + New ratio group sits under the last group.
  • Items without a ratio are gathered in a “No ratio” group, so none are forgotten.
The same group on the picture

On the picture

“I have” is shown big, in the centre, with your stock. Below, “What I want” lists one box per ratio, each with the picture of the item you give between the two numbers.

The stamp at the bottom (“Mix accepted” by default) and every title can be changed in Style.

TipWith many items, the boxes spread over more columns: the picture gets wider, not endless.

05Selling & Buying posts

Each item card reads like a sum: price each × quantity = total. Buying posts work exactly the same way.

The header

List name, number of items and the post total in coins ≈ rings · blankets. A −10% tag appears when a discount is on.

Each card

  1. Picture, name, ★, ⋯. Tap the name to rename the item.
  2. Price each — the price for one item.
  3. Unit button — tap to switch coins → 💍 rings → blankets → ×MP.
  4. Quantity — with − / + or by typing.
  5. = total in coins, then ≈ the same in rings · blankets.
  6. + Note — a small line under the item on the picture (“Fresh harvest”).

×MP items show one more line: Max price 7 · 5×MP = 35 each.

06Money rules: rings, blankets & ×MP

These values are built into the site and locked, so every post on the server counts the same way.

Diamond rings
10 💍 = 8,244

So 1 ring = 824.4 coins. This rate is fixed: there is no setting to change it.

Blankets
10 blankets = 10,980

So 1 blanket = 1,098 coins — the game’s max price for blankets. Fixed too.

  • Coins come first; rings and blankets are shown as “≈”.
  • A price in rings or blankets is turned into coins for the totals.
  • The big total can be in coins, rings or blankets, and each “≈” can be switched off (Discount & total).
×MP — times the max price

MP = the max price: the highest price the game lets you ask for an item in your roadside shop. 5×MP = five times that price.

Example

Corn: max price 7
5×MP → 5 × 7 = 35 coins each
300 Corn → 300 × 35 = 10,500 coins ≈ 12.7 💍

  • Where max prices come from: a built-in table for crops and fruits, and the Hay Day wiki for everything else (loaded by itself).
  • Expansion sets (BEM, SEM, LEM, TEM): the sum of their 3 materials.
  • Locked: nobody can type a max price, so nobody can bend ×MP prices.
  • If a max price can’t be found, the card says “max price not available”: use coins or rings for that item. Custom items can’t use ×MP.

10Order calculator

A buyer sends you a list? Paste it: the site finds each item in your post, uses your prices and gives the total in coins ≈ rings · blankets — with a reply ready for Discord.

  1. Open the Order tab. It uses the post that is open (here “Selling”).
  2. Paste the message as it is (Paste button or long press). One item per line. All these work: carrot pies x26 x 42 26x 26 carrot pie carrot pie: 26, with bullets or emojis.
  3. Each line is matched to an item of your post, even with plurals or short names (pies, rasp), and priced with your price: coins, rings or ×MP.
  4. Check the marks.
    ! not in your post → not counted. Tap the name to pick the right item, or ignore the line.
    ? several items could match → the best guess is counted; tap to check.
    You can correct any quantity.
  5. Read the total in coins ≈ rings ≈ blankets. If your post has a discount, a switch applies it.
  6. Copy the reply for Discord and paste it to the buyer:
26× Carrot Pie — 450 ea = 11,700
42× Pumpkin Pie — 900 ea = 37,800
21× Cotton Shirt — 1 ring ea = 17,312
30× Lobster Tail — 1,500 ea = 45,000
200× Corn — 5×MP (35 ea) = 7,000
**Total: 118,812 coins ≈ 144 💍 · ≈ 108 blankets**

An item you don’t sell

“golden egg” is not in the post, so it is marked ! and not counted. Tap its name: pick the item it should be, or Ignore this line.

Trade posts

The calculator uses your ratios. 89 Lemon Curd at 50 : 89 → you give 50; 178 Lobster Tail at 60 : 89 → you give 120. Total: 170 BEM Sets.

The suggestion then lowers your “I have” stock: 200 BEM Sets → 30 left.

12Long lists & saving

Up to 150 items per post, with tools to find your way.

  • Filter my items shows from 7 items. It hides everything that doesn’t match, without deleting anything.
  • ↑ ↓ buttons jump to the top or the bottom of the page. They appear while you scroll, then fade so they don’t cover your items.
  • The search bar stays at the top, so you can add an item from anywhere in the list.
  • The picture adapts: past 12 items it uses several columns and grows wider, not longer.

Saving & privacy

  • Automatic saving. Every change is saved right away in your browser. There is no Save button.
  • On your device only. When someone else opens the link, they get their own empty copy. Nobody can see or change your posts.
  • Your pictures (uploads) stay on your device too.
Be carefulClearing your browser data, or using a private window, erases your posts. Make a backup from time to time: Style → Backup → Export.

New phone or computer? Export the backup on the old one, then Import it on the new one.
