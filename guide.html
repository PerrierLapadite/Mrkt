3Trade posts

No prices: what you offer, what you want, and a ratio.

50 :89
Lemon Curd★⋯
Mayonnaise★⋯
＋ Add items here
50 BEM for 89 Lemon Curd
  • 50 : 89 = you give 50 of your item for 89 of theirs. The first number is always yours.
  • 1 set = 89 materials: 30 Bolt · 30 Plank · 29 Duct Tape. 10 sets = 890 = 300 · 300 · 290; what's left over is split evenly (134 BEM = 45 · 45 · 44). Same for SEM, LEM, TEM and Tools.
  • Your “I have” stock of a set is in sets (“BEM Set ×120” = 120 sets = 10,680 materials). After a deal of 890 BEM it goes to 110.
  • Items with the same ratio share one box. Change the big numbers → the whole box changes.
  • Bulk ratio (⋯ of a box): a better ratio for big deals — e.g. 10+ sets → 89 : 40 instead of 89 : 45. It shows on the picture, and Order uses it as soon as the deal reaches 10 sets.

I have

  • What you offer, with your stock (− / +). You can offer several items.
  • With several, tap the small picture in a ratio box to choose which one you give (⇄).

Choose where new items go

  • Pick the box first, then add items. The chosen box has a red frame.
  • + Ratio: type you give : you get, then Use this ratio.
  • ＋ Add items here (inside a box) does the same in one tap.
  • ⋯ of a box: add items, or remove the box with its items.
  • Items without a ratio wait in a “No ratio” box.

Add a deal

  • From Order or Check a post (Inventory): ⏳ Save for later. The box empties so you can handle the next one right away — nothing changes yet.
  • By hand: ＋ New deal → Trade, Sale or Purchase · the partner's name · what you give and what you get · Once or Every day for how many days (e.g. 89 BEM a day for 7 days).

Trades work with ratios

  • A trade = the items they give you, each with its ratio: 50 BEM : 89 = you give 50 BEM for 89 of theirs. You give is calculated.
  • Ratios come from your trade post (pick it under “Ratios from”). An item not in your post takes the ratio of the line before — change it if needed.
  • Right on the card: change a quantity or a ratio, or ＋ Add an item they give you — the total updates at once (whole items; sets in materials: 100 BEM = 34 Bolt · 33 Plank · 33 Duct Tape).
  • When it's done, your “I have” in the trade post goes down by what you really gave.

Follow them

  • Each card shows what you give and get. Red = your inventory doesn't have it any more; orange = you have it, but not for all your open deals together.
  • Every-day trades: Day 3 / 7, Today ✓ or Today: to do, and how many more days your inventory covers.
  • ✓ Done (or ✓ Today done): saved in History and Stats, your inventory follows, and the post it came from is lowered like in Order. Undo appears right after.
  • ⋯ → Edit, Copy or Cancel. Finished and cancelled deals stay at the bottom (you can reopen them). The red number on the Deals tab = open deals.
  • ✎ In Order: the deal opens in Order — change quantities, prices, lines. A bar on top shows it (Save, Back to Deals, Switch ⇄ to another deal). Leaving with changes asks which ones to keep: tick some, all, or drop them. ✓ Mark as done there finishes the deal.

11Profile & Inventory

Update your inventory from 🎬 a video, 📷 screenshots, 📝 a text or ＋ by hand — all four in Profile → Inventory. The round button at the top right opens your profile menu right where you are: your pseudo, Inventory, Stats, History, Backup and this guide. Tap outside, tap the button again or tap × to close it.

  • Pseudo: type it once — it becomes the username on all your pictures.

Your inventory from a screen recording (fastest)

1Open your Barn in the game. Start the iPhone's Screen Recording (Control Centre), scroll the barn slowly from the top to the bottom, stop. Same for the Silo (in the same video or another one).
2Profile → Inventory → 🎬 A video: pick the video. It is read while it plays (about as long as the video), on your phone.
3Check the list: each item with its number, in the barn's order. Orange = to check. Total = the number at the top of your Barn (3501 / 4450)? Then everything was read right. Save.
  • Every square is seen many times while it scrolls: its number is taken by vote, its item from its best views. A tooltip that pops up when you touch an item doesn't matter.
  • Fast scroll, going back up, closing and opening again, the farm before or after: all fine — an item is never counted twice.
  • A number hidden or unreadable everywhere: the item is still listed, in orange, with “?” — type how many you have.
  • Items at 0 in the barn are set to 0 in your inventory.
  • Use Replace my barn when the video shows the whole barn: the items that aren't in it any more are removed.

Your inventory from screenshots

1In the game, open your Barn and take a screenshot. Scroll, take another, until you've seen everything. Same for the Silo.
2Profile → Inventory → 📷 Screenshots: pick them all at once.
3Check the list: each picture, the item found and its number. Orange = to check. Then Save.
  • Everything is read on your phone — your screenshots are never sent anywhere.
  • Use the original screenshots (not cropped or resized). The numbers are read exactly; the item is found from its picture.
  • Wrong item? Tap it and pick the right one: the site learns from your fixes and gets it right next time.
  • Update these items changes only what was read. Replace my barn (or silo) when your screenshots show all of it.

Several farms

  • ＋ Farm adds a farm (each has its own barn and silo). Tap a farm to see it, tap it again to rename or delete it.
  • All farms = everything added up (each line says how much is in which farm). To change a number, open that farm.
  • Screenshots and new items go to the farm you picked. Choose which farm your deals change (“Deals change …”). Search and Items can look in all farms or only some.

By hand, and live

  • 📝 A text: paste or type a list, one item per line (or commas): Bolt x196, carrot pie 26, 30 plank. You get the same check list as with a video: fix, untick, then Update these items or Replace. “Copy the list” on another phone gives this format.
  • ＋ By hand: search an item and tap it (with how many).
  • Filter Barn / Silo, search, sort, change a number with − / +, × to remove. Copy the list gives “Bolt x196…”.
  • Update it with my deals (on by default): a deal marked as done in Order or Search changes your inventory — sold goes out, bought or received comes in. Sets (BEM…) change their materials.

12Import an old post

Your posts from before (on Discord, or made with another post maker) → a new post here, made and opened by itself. The ⤓ button at the top right, next to your profile.

1📷 Screenshots of the post — your Discord message (text, emojis, ~~struck~~ lines) and/or the picture of another post maker (tiles with “5x” and the quantity). Or paste the text: Discord → long-press the message → Copy Text.
2A new post of the right kind opens by itself — Trade (ratios), Buying (“buying”, “need”, “wtb”) or Selling — named after the author (“Trade · Ladloq”). Its tab is shown at the top and a note tells you it's the import. A trade and a picture of items for sale → two posts.
3Tap the orange ? Pick the item lines (pictures it didn't recognise) and choose the item. A wrong item: ⋯ → Change the item.
  • A picture made with Market Manager (the file, or a screenshot of it on Discord): it is read as a table — every item with its price, ×MP, quantity, rings or blankets, checked with the totals printed on it. Also its title, your name, the discount, the bulk bonus tiers, the notes and the line at the bottom. A number misread is put back from the others (price × quantity = total). Trade pictures: what you offer and each ratio box with its items. A name written too small to read → the item is found from its picture, or marked ? with the name already in the search.
  • Trades: Need 💍 50:89 under Have BEM = they give 50 rings for 89 BEM → here “you give 89 BEM : you get 50”.
  • Struck lines (70:89) come in as out of stock (⊘ to change it).
  • Emojis become the items — a saw comes in as Saw. Your picks are remembered: the next import recognises those pictures by itself.
  • Words it doesn't know as one game item stay as written: Fp jams, mussel foods, olive dip become items with that name.
  • Sets: Have 120 bem set with the 3 material emojis = BEM Set ×120, even when the reader misreads “bem”. A bulk ratio read far from the main one is not used — its text stays in the note so you can check it (⋯ → Bulk ratio).
  • Quantities and splits: Have 120 bem set (29/30/30) → BEM Set ×120, note “29/30/30”.
  • Bulk ratios: 45:89 // 40:89 for 10 sets + → 89 : 45, and 89 : 40 from 10 sets. No bulk discount → the note. Selling: 10% off → the discount. Buying: +10% for 100+ items → a bulk bonus.
  • Faces and stickers after a ratio (50:89 😶) are left out.
  • Other lines (“Dm & ping”, “Partial 50+”) become the notes of the post.
  • Everything is read on your phone; the text reader loads the first time (internet needed). Use the original screenshots. If a word is misread, fix it in the list — or paste the text instead.

14Style

How the picture looks. It never changes your items. Open it from the ☰ menu. With the Custom layout you choose rows or cards, the number of columns, the text size, and whether the quantity and the “1,500 ea” are shown.

  • Layout: Auto (best choice), Compact, List, Grid ×2, Grid ×3 (price tags).
  • Order of the items: Manual, Qty, Value or A–Z.
  • Texts: title, your Discord name, subtitle, “I have” heading, list heading, stamp (“DM to buy”), footer.
  • Post: change its type (Trade / Selling / Buying), its name, Duplicate or Delete it.
  • Backup: Export saves all your posts and History in one file; Import brings them back. Reset this post starts again (your name is kept).

16Good to know

  • ×MP max prices come from the game and can’t be changed.
